PAIRWISE HUDSON FST  —  predictive equation from this project's PCA
======================================================================

FORMULA
-------
  predicted_FST  =  1.518927  ×  d²  +  -0.004587

where
  d²  =  squared Euclidean distance between the two populations'
        geometric-median centroids in the top-20 PC space
        (geometric median is the point minimizing the sum of
        Euclidean distances to all members; robust to outliers,
        following Privé et al. 2022's approach)

HOW TO USE
----------
  Given two populations P and Q, each with a set of sample-level
  PC scores (top 20 PCs in the same coordinate system as this
  project's PCA):

    1. centroid_P = geometric median of P-samples' top-N PC vectors
    2. centroid_Q = geometric median of Q-samples' top-N PC vectors
    3. d²        = sum_{i=1..N} (centroid_P[i] - centroid_Q[i])²
    4. predicted_FST = a × d² + b   (using a, b above)

  Centroids for known populations and supervised groups are saved as:
    pca/centroids_top20_pop.tsv
    pca/centroids_top20_supervised.tsv
